# My Favorite Movie

## Everything Everywhere All at Once (2022)

### About the Movie

**Everything Everywhere All at Once** is a science fiction, action, and comedy film directed by Daniel Kwan and Daniel Scheinert.

The movie explores the concept of multiple universes, different versions of ourselves, family relationships, and the meaning of existence.

### Why I Love This Movie

I sometimes fail to notice that I tend to disregard others through my own thinking, just to validate what I believe is right. Others who turn out to be important—people who would cherish me. I unconsciously exhaust them to the point where letting go becomes the only option left in their sight.

> You could always try to save the whole universe, yet fail to see the singularity causing it to crumble.

### Final Thoughts

Perhaps we spend so much of our lives trying to make sense of everything everywhere, forgetting that sometimes, what matters most is right in front of us.

*Not everything needs to be extraordinary to be meaningful.*
